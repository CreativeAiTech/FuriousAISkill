**Changes**
- `rules/topics/overhead.md`: new section "No unmetered automation" after "Before adding start/end work". Any new automatic per-action write, push, hook or spawn ships with a counter in the project's daily registry.

**Why**: owner 2026-11-02: 300 bookkeeping commits a day on main and per-call hooks were found only after they hurt. A daily trend over counters finds a cost that grows faster than use; this rule keeps every new automation inside that count.

**Measure**: every PR that adds automatic work names its counter.
