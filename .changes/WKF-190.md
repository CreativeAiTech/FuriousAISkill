**Changes**
- `rules/topics/overhead.md`: new section "No unmetered automation". Any new automatic per-action write, push, hook or spawn ships with a counter in the project's daily cost trend registry (Etherium: WKF-190 `tools/costRadar.mjs`, `.agents/reports/metrics.jsonl`).

**Why**: owner 2026-11-02: 300 bookkeeping commits a day on main and per-call hooks were found only after they hurt. A daily trend over counters finds a cost that grows faster than use; this rule keeps every new automation inside that count.

**Measure**: every new automatic write, push, hook or spawn PR names its counter; the Etherium `cost-trend-radar` registry entry lists the counters it tracks.
